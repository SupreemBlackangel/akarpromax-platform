## Absolute separation

Paid promotion must remain separate from:

- verification truth

- GOLD

- review authenticity
- organic ranking

## Labeling rule

Sponsored content must be explicitly labeled.

## Product distinction

- Product = real catalog entity
- Sponsored Product = real product with paid visibility
