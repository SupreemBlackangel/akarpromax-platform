# Product / Supplier Marketplace Readiness

REQUIRES HUMAN LEGAL REVIEW BEFORE PRODUCTION

## Organization capability direction

Future business capabilities should be modeled as organization capabilities, not separate account types.

Examples:

- REAL_ESTATE
- PROVIDES_SERVICES
- SELLS_PRODUCTS
- MANUFACTURING
- DISTRIBUTION
- CONTRACTING
- ENGINEERING

## Business presence readiness

The public organization/business-presence surface should remain extensible for:

- about
- services
- properties
- products
- projects
- portfolio
- branches
- team
- certificates / claims
- contact / RFQ CTA
