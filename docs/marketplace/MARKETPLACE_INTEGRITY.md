- fake reviews
- review rings
- fake service requests
- fake provider identities
- misleading qualifications
- property fraud signals
- spam / harassment

## Enforcement model

Signal -> Review -> Action

## Trust rule

Advertising or future commercial payment must never directly buy:

- verification truth
- GOLD
- PROMAX
- fake ranking or fake reviews
