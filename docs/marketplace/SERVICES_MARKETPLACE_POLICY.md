# Services Marketplace Policy

REQUIRES HUMAN LEGAL REVIEW BEFORE PRODUCTION

## Current live lifecycle

Request -> Matching -> Offer -> Acceptance/Decline -> Job -> Provider completion -> Customer confirmation -> Review / Dispute

## Role separation

- Customer: publishes requests and chooses offers
- Professional / Service Company: publishes capability and submits offers
- AkarProMax: operates workflow, trust, moderation, and communications infrastructure

## Completion policy

- provider may mark a job complete
- customer confirmation finalizes verified completion
- dispute/report flows remain separate moderation mechanisms
