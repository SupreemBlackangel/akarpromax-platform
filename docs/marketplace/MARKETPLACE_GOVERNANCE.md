# Marketplace Governance

REQUIRES HUMAN LEGAL REVIEW BEFORE PRODUCTION

## Platform scope

AkarProMax currently operates:

- a real-estate marketplace
- a services marketplace
- a business / supplier ecosystem readiness layer

## Governance principles

- Trust is earned, not purchased.
- Sponsored visibility must remain separate from organic trust.
- Verification, reputation, and activity are separate systems.
- Platform moderation must distinguish weak signals from confirmed abuse.

## Runtime integrity already enforced

- request -> offer -> accept/decline -> job -> completion -> review lifecycle
- service dispute/report targets
- AMRS verification records
- organization ownership and member boundaries

## Required human review

- final legal language
- dispute/escalation obligations
- governing law / jurisdiction
- regulatory notices
